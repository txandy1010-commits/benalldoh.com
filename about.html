ABOUT BENALLDOH

BenAllDoh is a content creator and Twitch streamer based in Canada that primarily streams Geometry Dash. He officially started his current channel on December 28, 2025.

Born on March 16, 2011, BenAllDoh has had an interest in YouTube since the age of 4. When he was 6, inspired by other YouTubers like DanTDM and PopularMMOs, he started his first YouTube channel, where he obtained 600 subscribers.

When he was 12, he started a CapCut account, helping him learn about short form content and how to make it. The CapCut account gathered 25,000+ followers before he quit. When BenAllDoh was 14, he started streaming on Twitch, where all of this started.

Family Support

MomAllDoh, Ben's mother, has supported him throughout his journey. She spent time making Ben's profile picture for his first channel, and has stayed engaged and intrigued in all of Ben's social media accounts over the past decade.

BroAllDoh has been watching his brother for the past 10 years too, and BenAllDoh can recall BroAllDoh laughing at his video, which really motivated Ben at the time.

DadAllDoh has also contributed, helping Ben make thumbnails and descriptions when he was 7.
