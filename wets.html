The WETS Story

Created August 14, 2026

LIVE SYNC ACTIVE

WETS (Water Enthusiast Twitch Streamers) is a streamer collective co-founded by BenAllDoh alongside four other streamers: KingTheGreat_, SeannnnGD_, gloininkk_, and GD_Lumo.

The group was officially founded on August 14, 2026. The name was inspired by the viral "Eat Water" meme created by KingTheGreat_ on August 13, 2026. These five creators took advantage of the meme's popularity to create a unified team where viewers could enjoy all five streams in one spot!

Their debut stream on August 15, 2026 peaked at 67 concurrent viewers, generated 51 paid subscribers, and received 3,030 bits! Their top supporter L1medd contributed 42 paid subscriptions and 2,000 bits. On August 16, 2026, the team hit 100 followers following an announcement on BenAllDoh's livestream.
